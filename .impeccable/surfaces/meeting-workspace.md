## Speaker timeline

Colored speaker lanes show timed speech and shares of transcribed speech; silence is excluded, same-speaker overlaps count once, and simultaneous speakers each retain their speech. Actual speaker names remain accompanied by “Speaker separation is provisional.” Clicking a lane or using Left/Right and Home/End follows an exact passage, clears transcript search and seeks already loaded audio without autoplay. Audio position updates stay local to the timeline. Voice notes and transcripts without valid timed speech omit the dock.
